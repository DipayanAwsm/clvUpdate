# Client Pitch

## What This Platform Does
This CLV platform provides a complete journey from raw customer data to executive-ready actions.

## The Story We Walk Through
1. **We start with your base customer data** and validate quality, coverage, and shape.
2. **We run EDA** to uncover CLV distribution, concentration, and early risk patterns.
3. **We engineer behavioral features** (RFM + service/renewal signals) to capture true customer dynamics.
4. **We perform feature selection** with multiple techniques to isolate the strongest predictors.
5. **We benchmark several models** for CLV prediction and high-value customer identification.
6. **We select final models objectively** based on performance metrics.
7. **We deploy prediction endpoints and dashboard UI** for real-time and batch scoring.
8. **We convert predictions into action plans** for budget allocation and customer prioritization.

## Why Clients Buy This
- Faster and more confident retention decisions.
- Better allocation of marketing and service spend.
- Clear explanation of model decisions for stakeholder trust.
- Production-style API and dashboard ready for enterprise proof-of-concept.

## How Teams Use It
- Marketing: target high expected lifetime value customers with focused investment.
- Sales: prioritize accounts with strong growth and upsell potential.
- Service: intervene earlier for high-value accounts with risk signals.
- Leadership: monitor portfolio value mix and optimize investment strategy.
